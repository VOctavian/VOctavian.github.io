Approach

What I'm like to work with.

Six habits that have held up across every team and codebase so far.

Boring in production is the goal

Clever code is a liability at 3am. I optimize for the version a tired teammate can read and change safely six months from now.

Measure, then touch

"It feels slow" isn't a diagnosis. Profile, find the actual query, fix that one thing. Most big wins are one index and one N+1.

Legacy isn't the enemy

It's revenue that already works. I refactor in seams, behind tests, without a rewrite that stops the roadmap for a quarter.

Async-first communication

Years of remote work with distributed teams. Written clarity, small PRs, decisions in the open — nobody has to share a timezone to move.

Reviews are teaching, not gatekeeping

Leading a team taught me the review is where standards actually get set. I explain the why, and I'm happy to be the one corrected.

AI is a power tool, not a pilot

I use agentic tooling daily to move faster on scaffolding, tests and refactors — and I still read every line before it merges.
